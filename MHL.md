### **Part IV: The Foundational Mandates (The Axiomatic Laws of Physical Sovereignty)**

These mandates are non-negotiable, axiomatic design requirements. Any Hardware manufactured from a Design licensed under MHL-1.0 must fully and verifiably comply with them. Failure by a Commercial Distributor to implement these mandates constitutes a material breach of this License and a forfeiture of rights.

1.  **The Mandate of Absolute User Control (Anti-Tivoization & Anti-Bricking):** This License explicitly and irrevocably forbids any mechanism—whether technical, cryptographic, or legal—that prevents, obstructs, or places an unreasonable burden (including but not limited to requiring online authentication, proprietary tools, or non-public keys) on a User's ability to compile the Corresponding Source and install and run modified versions of the Necessary Software on The Hardware. Any attempt by a Commercial Distributor to render The Hardware inoperable ("bricking") due to "unauthorized" modification or repair is an egregious material breach. This breach results in the immediate and permanent termination of all rights under this License for the violating party and shall expose them to legal action for damages and injunctive relief.
2.  **The Mandate of Radical Repairability:** The Design must be architected to facilitate, not hinder, repair and disassembly. This includes:
    *   **Prohibition on Hostile Architectures:** The use of non-standard fasteners where standard ones suffice, the application of excessive adhesives that prevent non-destructive disassembly, and the practice of parts-pairing (where components are cryptographically serialized and locked to a specific device to prevent replacement) are all deemed hostile to the User and are a direct violation of this mandate.
    *   **Availability of Complete Diagnostics:** The Design must include all schematics, protocols, and documentation for any diagnostic ports, JTAG/SWD interfaces, or software endpoints necessary to troubleshoot, debug, and repair The Hardware at a component level.
3.  **The Mandate of Supply Chain Transparency & Resilience:** The Bill of Materials (BoM) must, to the greatest extent commercially feasible, specify components that are standardized, well-documented, and available from multiple public sources. The deliberate use of proprietary, single-source, or artificially scarce components for the purpose of hindering repair, modification, or independent manufacture is a violation of this mandate.
4.  **The Mandate of Verifiable Privacy (Hardware Kill Switches):** The Design *must* include specifications for physical, user-controllable hardware switches that electrically and verifiably disconnect key sensors and transceivers from the main processor and power supply. The state of each switch must be verifiable by a corresponding physical indicator (e.g., an LED) whose circuit is directly tied to the switch state. This mandate applies to, at a minimum:
    *   All microphones.
    *   All cameras.
    *   All wireless transceivers (e.g., Wi-Fi, Bluetooth, Cellular, NFC).
    *   All location-tracking receivers (e.g., GPS/GNSS).
5.  **The Mandate of Cryptographic Sovereignty (Hardware Security Features):** The Design *must* include specifications for the following hardware capabilities to ensure the User has the physical tools necessary to achieve cryptographic sovereignty:
    *   A **Hardware True Random Number Generator (TRNG)** that meets or exceeds industry standards (e.g., NIST SP 800-90B) for generating high-entropy cryptographic randomness.
    *   **Cryptographic Acceleration** for, at minimum, AES-256 and SHA-256 algorithms to make robust, high-performance encryption a default, low-overhead capability.
    *   An **Isolated Secure Key Storage Element** (e.g., a Trusted Platform Module, Secure Enclave, or equivalent) capable of generating, storing, and managing cryptographic keys in a manner that isolates them from the main operating system and all other software.
6.  **The Mandate of Post-Mortem Sovereignty:** Upon the death or legal dissolution of the Licensor, if no specific legal heir or successor foundation for the copyright has been publicly and explicitly designated, the copyright and all other intellectual property rights to The Design shall automatically and irrevocably transition into a perpetual, unencumbered public trust, managed by a steward designated by MitsuoLabs or its successor, exclusively under the terms of this License. This is to prevent valuable designs from becoming "orphan works" or being captured by entities hostile to the principles of this License.
7.  **The Mandate of License Primacy for Software:** In the event of a direct conflict between the terms of this License (MHL-1.0) and the license of any Necessary Software for which The Design is hardware (e.g., if the Necessary Software is licensed under MRSL-1.0 or GPLv3), the terms of the software license shall take precedence *only for the software itself*. The Design and The Hardware remain governed exclusively by MHL-1.0.
8.  **The Mandate of Planetary Responsibility:** Any Commercial Distributor, by the act of Conveying The Hardware, accepts an affirmative duty of environmental stewardship. This includes a good-faith commitment to:
    *   **Ethical Sourcing:** Performing due diligence on their supply chain to minimize the use of materials sourced from conflict zones or through exploitative labor practices.
    *   **Lifecycle Assessment:** Measuring and publicly reporting, on an annual basis, the estimated lifecycle emissions and resource consumption associated with the manufacturing of The Hardware.
