## Why This Framework Exists: A New Covenant for a Converged Age

The legal tools of the 20th century are failing the converged realities of the 21st. The distinctions between code, content, and physical matter are collapsing. Existing licenses are ill-equipped for artificial intelligence, sovereign hardware, decentralized systems, and the existential risks of unaccountable technology. The MitsuoLabs™ Legal Framework was created to fill this void.

It is not merely a set of licenses; it is a **moral and legal operating system** designed to ensure that the technologies that shape our future are bound by principles of justice, stewardship, and human dignity. It is for builders who recognize that **invention without stewardship is negligence**, and for communities that demand a more resilient and equitable commons for all forms of creation.

## The Core Architecture: A Unified Framework for Reality

This framework provides a holistic, interlocking architecture to govern the fundamental domains of modern creation and commerce.

### The Source Trinity: A Foundation for Ethical Creation

The foundation of the framework is a trinity of share-alike licenses that govern the source of all creation, ensuring it remains perpetually open and ethically bound.

*   **The MitsuoLabs Content & Architecture License (MCAL-1.0):** For the sovereignty of **Knowledge**. Governs datasets, academic papers, books, and all forms of content.
*   **The MitsuoLabs Reciprocity & Stewardship License (MRSL-1.0):** For the sovereignty of **Code**. Governs software source code.
*   **The MitsuoLabs Hardware License (MHL-1.0):** For the sovereignty of **Matter**. Governs schematics, CAD files, and physical designs.

### The Path to Market: The Dual-License Model for Software

For software, the framework’s core innovation is a dual-license structure that cleanly separates the governance of source code from the governance of compiled binaries. This allows a project to be both **truly FOSS at its foundation** and **commercially sound in its distribution**.

```
  [ SOURCE CODE ]
        |
(Governed by MRSL-1.0)
 (Freedom & Ethics)
        |
     compiles
        |
        v
[ EXECUTABLE BINARY ]
        |
 (Governed by MMPEULA-1.0)
(Professional & Commercial Use)

```

*   **The MitsuoLabs Master Professional EULA (MMPEULA-1.0):** A professional-grade, legally hardened End-User License Agreement designed for commercializing compiled software while providing unprecedented clarity and fairness to professional users.

## Who Is This Framework For?

*   **For the Principled Enterprise:** Build a competitive moat based on ethics and trust. Use the **MRSL-1.0** for your source to attract community talent, and distribute the compiled product under the **MMPEULA-1.0** to provide the professional-grade legal armor you need to commercialize with confidence.

*   **For the Open-Source Maintainer:** Protect your code from exploitation with the **MRSL-1.0**. Its reciprocity clauses ensure derivatives remain open, while its ethical covenants prevent your work from being used for harm.

*   **For the AI Researcher & Dataset Curator:** Govern training data with unprecedented clarity using the **MCAL-1.0**. Its AI-specific clauses for provenance and fairness ensure your datasets build a more responsible AI ecosystem.

*   **For the Open Hardware Designer:** Liberate your users from black-box devices with the **MHL-1.0**. Codify the Right to Repair, mandate hardware kill switches, and demand planetary responsibility from manufacturers.

## Quick Start Guide

1.  **For Your Source Code (MRSL-1.0):**
    *   To guarantee your code remains free and ethically bound, place `mrsl-1.0.txt` in your repository as `LICENSE`.

2.  **For Your Content & Datasets (MCAL-1.0):**
    *   To license articles, books, or AI training data, place `mcal-1.0.txt` in your repository as `LICENSE`.

3.  **For Your Hardware Designs (MHL-1.0):**
    *   To license schematics and CAD files, place `mhl-1.0.txt` in your repository as `LICENSE`.

4.  **For Your Compiled Software (MMPEULA-1.0):**
    *   To distribute your compiled software with professional-grade legal protection, use `mmpeula-1.0.txt` as your EULA template.
