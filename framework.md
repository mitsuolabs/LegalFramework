# The MitsuoLabs™ Legal Framework: A Unified Overview

---

## The Vision: A New Operating System for Digital Trust

The MitsuoLabs™ Legal Framework is a complete, integrated legal and ethical operating system for the entire lifecycle of a project—from the first line of source code to global commercial distribution, and extending to the knowledge and physical devices that surround it. It was created to address the fundamental failure of 20th-century legal tools to govern 21st-century technology.

This framework is not a collection of disparate licenses; it is a single, coherent system designed to ensure that technology remains free, ethical, and commercially robust.

## The Core Architecture: A Trifecta of Sovereignty

The framework’s power derives from its integrated architecture, which cleanly and logically governs the three realms of modern creation: software, knowledge, and hardware.

### **1. The Foundation: The MitsuoLabs™ Reciprocity and Stewardship License (MRSL-1.0)**

The MRSL-1.0 governs the **source code**. It is a next-generation, strong-copyleft license designed to guarantee that the code and all its derivatives remain perpetually free and open.

*   **Key Function:** To build and protect an ethical, open-source commons.
*   **Read More:** [Key Innovations of the MRSL-1.0](./docs/mrsl/innovations.md)

### **2. The Superstructure: The MitsuoLabs™ Master Professional and End User License Agreement (MMPEULA-1.0)**

The MMPEULA-1.0 governs the **compiled binary**—the final product distributed to end-users. It is a professional-grade commercial EULA designed for maximum legal clarity and robustness.

*   **Key Function:** To provide a stable, predictable, and defensible legal framework for commercial distribution.
*   **Read More:** [Key Innovations of the MMPEULA-1.0](./docs/mmpeula/innovations.md)

## The Foundational Covenants: Extending the Framework

The dual-license architecture of MRSL and MMPEULA provides a complete lifecycle for software. However, the Legacy Mandate requires a framework that governs all forms of creation. The MitsuoLabs Covenants extend our legal operating system to the realms of knowledge and physical creation, ensuring that our principles are manifest in everything we build.

### **3. The Knowledge Covenant: The MitsuoLabs Universal Birthright License (MUBL-1.0)**

The MUBL-1.0 governs **content and data**. It is a next-generation, strong-copyleft license designed to ensure that knowledge and data remain perpetually free, open, and uncorruptible.

*   **Key Function:** To build and protect a universal, censorship-proof library of human knowledge.
*   **Core Innovation:** The **Anti-Censorship & Preservation Clause**, which makes any attempt to remove or obscure the work a direct violation of the license, and the **Data Sovereignty & Portability Clause**, which explicitly forbids data lock-in.
*   **Read More:** [The MitsuoLabs Universal Birthright License](./MUBL.md)

### **4. The Hardware Covenant: The MitsuoLabs Physical Stewardship License (MPSL-1.0)**

The MPSL-1.0 governs **physical hardware designs**. It is a next-generation, strong-copyleft license designed to guarantee a user's absolute stewardship over their physical devices.

*   **Key Function:** To combat planned obsolescence and empower users with the inalienable right to repair and control their own hardware.
*   **Core Innovation:** The **Anti-Tivoization & Anti-Bricking Mandate**, which makes it a material breach of the license for a manufacturer to prevent a user from repairing their device or installing modified firmware.
*   **Read More:** [The MitsuoLabs Physical Stewardship License](./MPSL.md)
