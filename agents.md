## 3. The Four Pillars of Governance: The Licenses

This framework provides a complete system for governing the modern creative stack: **Code, Content, Hardware, and Commerce.**

### 📜 `mrsl-1.0.txt` — The MitsuoLabs Reciprocity & Stewardship License

*   **Domain:** Source Code.
*   **Axiom:** **Code Must Remain Free and Ethically Bound.**
*   **Function:** A "strong copyleft" license. If you use MRSL-licensed code in your project, your project's source code must also be made available under the MRSL. It ensures that the commons cannot be privatized. Crucially, it contains non-negotiable **Ethical Use Covenants** that prohibit the use of the code for applications like autonomous weaponry or mass surveillance.

### 📚 `mcal.md` — The MitsuoLabs Content & Architecture License

*   **Domain:** Content & Data (Text, Images, Datasets).
*   **Axiom:** **Knowledge Must Be Verifiable and Accessible.**
*   **Function:** The MCAL governs all non-software information. It is designed for the age of AI, with clauses for data provenance, ensuring that the training data for machine learning models is transparent and its lineage is known. It guarantees that knowledge remains open and un-censorable.

### 🔩 `mhl.md` — The MitsuoLabs Hardware License

*   **Domain:** Physical Designs (Schematics, CAD files, 3D printer models).
*   **Axiom:** **You Have the Right to Understand and Control Your Own Devices.**
*   **Function:** The MHL is designed to fight against the plague of black-box, disposable hardware. It mandates that any physical object built from MHL designs must be repairable. It codifies the **Right to Repair** and user sovereignty over the physical world.

### 💼 `mmpeula-1.0.txt` — The MitsuoLabs Master Professional EULA

*   **Domain:** Commercial Distribution of Binaries.
*   **Axiom:** **Ethical FOSS Must Have a Viable Path to Market.**
*   **Function:** This is the bridge from the open-source commons to the world of professional commerce. The framework follows a **Dual-License Model**. While the source code is protected by the MRSL, you can distribute the compiled, executable version of your software under the MMPEULA. This professional-grade EULA is designed for B2B transactions, providing the legal clarity and protection necessary for a sustainable business, without compromising the freedom of the underlying source code.

## 4. The Moral Compass: Axiomatic Ethics in Practice

This framework is not morally neutral. It is engineered with a powerful and explicit ethical compass, designed to protect human dignity and prevent the exploitation inherent in much of modern technology. The licenses are not just legal instruments; they are moral declarations.

A core, non-negotiable principle woven into the **MRSL**, **MHL**, and **MMPEULA** is the **prohibition of dehumanizing technologies**. This is not a vague guideline; it is an axiomatic, legally-binding rule. For example, the framework explicitly forbids the use of its code, hardware designs, or software for:

*   **Creating Systems for Sexual Exploitation:** This includes a strict ban on creating robotic or AI-driven systems for erotic roleplay, sexual solicitation, or simulating sexual acts.
*   **Deploying Libidinous Personalization:** The framework prohibits using the technology to build user profiles or recommendation engines whose primary purpose is to engage users in a lustful or sexually explicit manner.
*   **Distributing Tools for Sexual Gratification:** The commercial distribution of tools designed for sexual self-gratification is considered a material breach of the EULA, reflecting a strong moral stance on the purpose of technology.

This is not about prudishness; it is about **stewardship**. It is a recognition that technology which commodifies or simulates human intimacy for gratification can be profoundly dehumanizing. A Legacy Architect builds tools that elevate humanity, not those that reduce it to a set of programmable responses. This is one of many such ethical firewalls built into the system.
